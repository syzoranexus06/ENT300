Our Facilities

Explore the proposed venue layout, gaming stations and support areas for the Syzora Nexus ENT300 business concept. Facilities are planned, not yet operational.

PROPOSED 1,200–1,500 SQ. FT. PREMISES

A comfortable space for gaming.

The Syzora Nexus business plan proposes eight standard console stations and one private PS5 room. The final fit-out, equipment specifications, accessibility and safety arrangements depend on the premises, supplier quotations and relevant approvals.

Four PS5 Stations

Four standard PlayStation 5 gaming stations are included in the proposed initial operating capacity. Planned hourly rental: RM15 per station.

Two PS4 Pro Stations

Two PlayStation 4 Pro stations provide an additional console option. Planned hourly rental: RM10 per station.

Two Nintendo Switch OLED Stations

Two Switch OLED stations are proposed for varied play styles and shared gaming sessions. Planned hourly rental: RM12 per station.

One Private PS5 Room

One independently bookable private room with a dedicated PS5 is planned for groups of up to four guests at RM30 per hour.

Reception & Booking Support

The proposed front-of-house area will support customer enquiries, booking checks, payment handling and communication of session rules.

Customer Waiting Area

A customer waiting and circulation zone is planned near reception. Seating, furniture and interior finishes will be determined during fit-out planning.

Ventilation & Power

Appropriate ventilation, reliable electrical supply and equipment cooling are operating requirements to validate for the selected shop lot.

Safety & Accessibility

Safe exit routes, accessible customer circulation and appropriate security measures will be considered. CCTV coverage, accessibility features and other measures are not yet confirmed.

Equipment Care & Storage

A support area is proposed for cleaning supplies and equipment checks between sessions. Maintenance procedures will be finalized before any operation.

CONCEPTUAL OPERATING LAYOUT

How the space is planned.

The following zones follow the proposed ENT300 operating layout. This is not a scaled architectural plan; dimensions, exits and fittings require site verification.

Zone 1 · Reception & Entrance

Proposed customer service counter for information, payment and booking enquiries, with a waiting area and visible house rules.

Zone 2 · PS5 Gaming Area

Four standard PS5 stations within the main gaming floor, subject to the final seating and electrical layout.

Zone 3 · PS4 Pro Gaming Area

Two standard PS4 Pro stations in the planned shared gaming floor.

Zone 4 · Nintendo Switch Area

Two Switch OLED stations as part of the eight planned standard stations.

Zone 5 · Private PS5 Room

One dedicated private gaming room for a maximum of four guests. No second private room or VIP suite is included in the initial plan.

Zone 6 · Staff Support & Storage

Proposed storage and cleaning-supplies area, with circulation and safe exits to be assessed after identifying the premises.

Proposed premises: approximately 1,200–1,500 sq. ft. in the candidate Seksyen 7, Shah Alam area. No shop lot, parking arrangement, internet service level or fit-out has been verified.
