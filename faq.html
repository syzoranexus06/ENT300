FAQ

Answers about the proposed Syzora Nexus Gaming Arena. Prices and services are planning estimates, not live commercial offers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Your questions, answered

These answers describe the approved proposed business plan. Operating arrangements, customer rules and website functionality require confirmation before launch.

Is Syzora Nexus currently open?+

No operating venue has been verified. Syzora Nexus Gaming Arena is a proposed business developed for a UiTM ENT300 academic business plan, and this website is a prototype.

Where will Syzora Nexus be located?+

Seksyen 7, Shah Alam, Selangor is the candidate area. A specific shop lot, address, rental quotation and relevant approvals have not yet been confirmed.

What are the proposed opening hours?+

The provisional schedule is 12:00 PM to 12:00 AM daily. These hours are planning assumptions, not confirmed public opening hours, and may change with licensing and staffing requirements.

Which consoles and stations are planned?+

The initial plan includes four standard PlayStation 5 stations, two PlayStation 4 Pro stations and two Nintendo Switch OLED stations, plus one separately bookable private room with a dedicated PS5. That is nine independently bookable units.

How much does a standard gaming session cost?+

Proposed standard hourly rates are RM15 for PS5, RM10 for PS4 Pro and RM12 for Nintendo Switch OLED. Multi-hour totals use those rates unless a future promotion is formally approved. See the Pricing page.

Is a private gaming room available?+

The business plan proposes one private PS5 room for up to four guests at RM30 per room per hour. Other VIP rooms and additional themed rooms are not part of the approved initial facility plan.

Can I make a booking on the website?+

The Booking page demonstrates a proposed reservation interface. A live reservation system, synchronized availability, payment processing and booking confirmations have not been verified or launched. Do not treat a prototype interaction as an actual booking.

Do you accept walk-in customers?+

Walk-in enquiries are part of the proposed customer service process, subject to station availability when the business becomes operational. No guaranteed walk-in allocation or current customer-traffic statistics have been established.

What membership plans are proposed?+

Bronze / Gaming Passport is free. Proposed Silver membership costs RM29 per month with a 5% console discount, seven-day booking priority and RM5 monthly console credit. Proposed Gold membership costs RM79 per month with a 10% console discount, 14-day booking priority and RM20 monthly console credit. Credits expire monthly, and promotions do not stack.

How would Gaming Passport points work?+

The proposed free loyalty programme awards one point per RM1 of eligible spending. Every 100 points can be redeemed for RM5 in console credit, and points expire after 12 months. Actual earning and redemption functions are not live.

Are tournaments and birthday packages available?+

Community tournaments and group activities are future concepts. No event dates, entry fees, prize pools, paid birthday packages or separate event revenue are confirmed in the approved financial model.

Can children participate, and are there age restrictions?+

The proposed primary market is students and young adults aged 18–30, with families and groups as secondary audiences. Exact minimum ages, guardian requirements and game-content restrictions must be established before operation; the website cannot confirm an admission policy yet.

Will there be food, parking or guest Wi-Fi?+

An in-house kitchen, partner food delivery, free parking, defined internet speeds and guest Wi-Fi service have not been confirmed in the initial plan. Facilities will depend on the selected premises, suppliers and final operations decisions.

What are the cancellation, refund and equipment rules?+

Final cancellation periods, deposits, refunds, use of personal controllers, damage handling and technical-downtime remedies have not been formally approved. Any binding policy should be published before real bookings or payments begin.

How can I ask a question or express interest?+

Use the Contact page to view the project contact information. Any contact details marked VERIFY or REPLACE must be confirmed by the group; response times, booking confirmations and customer-support availability are not guaranteed.

Still got questions?

See the proposed facilities, pricing and membership details, or visit the Contact page for project enquiries. No live customer support response time is promised.
