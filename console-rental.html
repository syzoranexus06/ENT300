HOW IT WORKS

From enquiry to game time: the proposed process.

Request a Slot

View the proposed website booking interface or make a walk-in enquiry once the venue opens. The demo does not guarantee real-time availability.

Choose a Station

Select PS5, PS4 Pro or Nintendo Switch OLED. Staff would confirm the selected station and available games.

Check In & Prepare

Staff would verify the booking or walk-in request, explain house rules, prepare the setup and check equipment.

Play & Complete Session

Play during the agreed period. Extensions depend on station availability; staff would complete checks before the next session.

HOUSE RULES

Proposed session guidelines.

  • Food and drink care
    Keep food and drinks away from gaming equipment; follow staff guidance for designated eating areas.
  • Respect the booked time
    Sessions are based on the agreed duration. Extra time would require staff approval and available capacity; no automatic grace period is assumed.
  • Use authorized games and services
    Only approved games and console services may be used. Game titles and multiplayer support are subject to availability and relevant rights.
  • Handle equipment responsibly
    Treat controllers, displays and furnishings carefully. Any damage-handling policy must be communicated and confirmed before operation.
  • Be considerate of others
    Respect staff, other players and the shared environment. Final venue rules will be published after operational review.
