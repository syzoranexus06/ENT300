Group Events

Explore possible group-gaming experiences for the proposed Syzora Nexus Gaming Arena. These are ideas for future development, not currently available event packages.

FUTURE SERVICE CONCEPTS

Gaming is better together.

The approved ENT300 plan focuses on hourly console rentals and one private PS5 room. Group and celebration packages may be considered later, but their feasibility, pricing, staffing and facilities have not yet been verified.

CONCEPT A · SMALL GROUP SESSION
Standard hourly console rates

An illustrative casual session using the proposed regular gaming stations.

  • Choose among 4 PS5, 2 PS4 Pro and 2 Switch OLED standard stations, subject to availability
  • Proposed rates: PS5 RM15/hour, PS4 Pro RM10/hour, Switch OLED RM12/hour
  • Players and suitable game titles depend on the selected setup
  • Food, decorations, giveaways and hosting are not included in the approved base offering
  • Separate group discounts or packages have not been finalized
View Proposed Console Rentals
CONCEPT B · PRIVATE ROOM SESSION
RM 30/ hour · proposed

A small-group gaming concept based on the single dedicated private PS5 room in the business plan.

  • 1 proposed private room with a dedicated PS5
  • Maximum of 4 guests in the room
  • 2 hours: RM60 at standard rate; 3 hours: RM90 at standard rate
  • No approved VIP suite, extra private rooms or large-party room capacity
  • Availability, booking procedures and any event services require confirmation before launch
View Proposed Private Room

Academic prototype note: the examples above illustrate regular hourly rental pricing. They are not event-package offers, reservations, quotations or a guarantee of service.

POSSIBLE COMMUNITY ACTIVITIES

Ideas for future group experiences.

These activities are potential concepts only. No scheduled dates, contracted partners, instructors, age-specific supervision, package prices or event budgets have been approved.

Birthday Gaming Sessions

Small birthday gatherings could be explored using standard stations or the four-guest private PS5 room. Cakes, catering and decorations would require separate arrangements and validation.

Friends & Student Groups

Informal gaming meetups for students and friends could complement regular rental sessions. Any special group rates would require a later pricing and capacity review.

Community Challenges

Small console challenges may be considered as community activities. Entry fees, prize pools and event dates are not confirmed and are excluded from the approved financial forecast.

Team Gaming

Cooperative or friendly competitive sessions could be designed for small groups if sufficient demand and staffing are established.

Seasonal Game Nights

Future themed gatherings are an idea for marketing and community engagement, not a published schedule or currently bookable activity.

Event Feasibility Review

Any future package would first need customer research, a safe-capacity assessment, supplier quotations, operating procedures and an approved selling price.
