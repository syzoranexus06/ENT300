Rules & Safety

Proposed customer guidelines for respectful gaming, equipment care and visitor safety. Final venue rules remain subject to approval.

Academic prototype notice: This website documents a proposed UiTM ENT300 business, not an operating gaming arena.

PROPOSED CODE OF CONDUCT

Respectful play. Shared responsibility.

These are draft customer guidelines for the proposed Syzora Nexus Gaming Arena, not rules currently being enforced at an operating venue. Exact admission, conduct, penalties and refund policies will require review before opening.

Respect for Everyone

Treat other visitors and staff respectfully. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks and intimidation would be addressed through a documented staff-led incident process.

Reasonable Noise Levels

Keep sound at a level appropriate for a shared gaming space. Staff would manage noise based on the actual room layout; no soundproofing performance is assumed.

Fair Play & Game Integrity

Follow the published rules for any future tournaments. Unauthorized software, disruptive conduct and tampering with gaming systems would not be permitted.

Clean Shared Equipment

Use controllers and other shared items responsibly. Staff would check and clean equipment between sessions as part of the proposed turnover process.

Age & Guardian Requirements

The proposed primary market is adults aged 18–30, with families as a secondary audience. Minimum admission ages, guardian supervision and game-content restrictions must be finalized before launch.

Smoke-Free Gaming Areas

The proposed indoor gaming areas would be kept free from smoking, vaping and alcohol consumption, subject to applicable rules and the final premises policy. No designated outdoor smoking area has been confirmed.

Protect the Equipment

Avoid throwing controllers, hitting screens, moving equipment without permission or otherwise causing damage. Any damage-assessment or reimbursement policy must be published before operation.

Food & Drink Care

Keep food and beverages away from sensitive gaming equipment unless staff explicitly allow otherwise. A snack bar or food-service partnership is not included as a confirmed initial offering.

Look After Your Belongings

Keep valuables with you and report missing items to staff. Lockers, personal-property custody services and related liability terms are not yet confirmed.

Photography & Privacy

Ask for consent before filming or photographing other visitors. Any surveillance, signage, data retention and image-handling arrangements would require an approved privacy policy.

Report Problems to Staff

Visitors would be encouraged to inform staff of harassment, unsafe behavior, faulty equipment or other concerns. Reporting channels and response procedures require confirmation.

Positive Gaming Community

Enjoy friendly competition, follow allocated session times and cooperate with staff and other guests. Tournaments and special events are future concepts, not currently scheduled services.

SAFETY & OPERATIONS PLANNING

Safer sessions by design.

The business plan anticipates reliable equipment, safe circulation, suitable ventilation and a structured staff-managed session process. These operational requirements remain proposed and must be verified for the actual premises.

Cleaning & Turnover

Proposed workflow includes cleaning and checking controllers, displays and seating between bookings. Detailed schedules, chemicals and staff assignments require final operational planning.

Ventilation & Comfort

Adequate airflow, temperature control and reasonable capacity must be assessed when premises are selected. HEPA filtration and specific air-quality performance are not confirmed.

Emergency Readiness

Staff would need a documented process for responding to incidents and contacting emergency services. First-aid training, supplies and any equipment are subject to verification.

Fire & Premises Safety

Final premises must meet applicable fire, evacuation, occupancy and local authority requirements. No inspection, certification or approved safety installation is claimed.

Staff Supervision

Staff would oversee customer sessions, monitor equipment condition and escalate safety concerns. Staffing qualifications, safeguarding procedures and shift coverage require confirmation.

Visitor Safeguarding

The proposed business should establish appropriate incident and safeguarding procedures, including specific arrangements for younger visitors if permitted. The details are not yet finalized.

Need more information?

For questions about this proposed community and safety approach, see the Contact page. Contact details, incident-reporting arrangements and response times must be verified before public launch.
