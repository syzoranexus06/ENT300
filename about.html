OUR STORY

Discover the concept behind a proposed console-gaming arena designed for shared play, convenient booking and community experiences.

OUR BUSINESS CONCEPT

Gaming together, made accessible.

Syzora Nexus Gaming Arena is a proposed partnership business developed for the UiTM ENT300 business plan. The idea is to offer a comfortable, customer-focused space for console gaming, social entertainment and group experiences.

The initial operating plan includes four standard PlayStation 5 stations, two PlayStation 4 Pro stations, two Nintendo Switch OLED stations and one private room with a dedicated PS5, for a total of nine independently bookable gaming units.

The proposed location is Seksyen 7, Shah Alam, Selangor. The site, facilities, operating hours and reservation system remain subject to confirmation and implementation. This website is a student-developed prototype, not an operational booking service.

Our Mission

To provide quality gaming facilities and customer-focused services through affordable console rentals, comfortable gaming environments, convenient digital reservations, and engaging gaming community activities.

Our Vision

To become a preferred gaming entertainment destination in Selangor by providing accessible, enjoyable, and digitally connected gaming experiences.
