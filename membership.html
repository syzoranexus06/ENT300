MEMBERSHIP GUIDELINES

How the proposed programme works.

Earn & Redeem Points

Gaming Passport members would earn 1 point per RM1 eligible spend. A balance of 100 points would be worth RM5 in console credit, with a proposed 12-month points expiry.

Priority Booking

Silver members would have a seven-day booking window; Gold members a fourteen-day window. Priority does not guarantee an available station.

Monthly Credits

Proposed monthly console credits are RM5 for Silver and RM20 for Gold. Unused monthly credits would expire and could not be withdrawn as cash.

Terms Pending

Promotions would not stack. Account registration, verification, payment and refund policies need to be finalized before any public membership launch.
