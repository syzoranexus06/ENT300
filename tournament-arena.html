PROPOSED EVENT PROCESS

How an event could work.

STEP 1

Assess Demand

Gather interest from potential customers and evaluate available equipment, space and staffing.

STEP 2

Set Event Rules

Confirm the selected game, player capacity, accessibility and safety arrangements before advertising.

STEP 3

Approve Budget

Evaluate costs, any proposed fees, benefits or prizes, and financial feasibility separately from base console-rental forecasts.

STEP 4

Publish Verified Details

Only after approval, announce the final date, eligibility, format, registration conditions and any applicable fees.
