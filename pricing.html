Gaming Passport points

Proposed free loyalty scheme: 1 point per RM1 of eligible spending; 100 points may be redeemed for RM5 console credit. Points expire after 12 months.

Membership conditions

Silver and Gold monthly credits expire at the end of each month and are not exchangeable for cash. Discounts and promotions do not stack. The Gold tournament concession is a proposed benefit, subject to events being organized.

OTHER IDEAS UNDER REVIEW

Future offers.

These items are not confirmed prices or promotions. Their feasibility must be checked against cost, demand and the approved financial model before being advertised as available.

Off-peak promotion

A possible 20% off-peak offer is mentioned in the business plan but remains subject to margin testing. No promotional hours or eligibility conditions have been approved.

Multi-hour packages

Proposed three-hour and ten-hour packages require further price and margin assessment. Standard hourly totals are shown above for illustration, without package discounts.

Tournaments & group events

Community tournaments and event packages are future concepts. There are no approved separate entry fees, birthday-package rates or event revenue streams in the current plan.

Food, beverages, streamer booths, additional private rooms and related price lists are not included as confirmed initial services in the approved proposal. No snack or drink menu is being offered on this prototype page.
