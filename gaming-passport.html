Gaming Passport

A proposed free, points-based loyalty programme for Syzora Nexus Gaming Arena. Earn points on eligible spending and exchange them for console credit. ENT300 academic prototype — programme not yet active.

PROPOSED FREE LOYALTY PROGRAMME

Your gaming journey, rewarded.

The Gaming Passport, also called the Bronze tier, is the free entry level of our proposed membership programme. Eligible console spending would earn points that can be redeemed for console credit once the minimum threshold is reached.

This is a planned reward system in the approved business plan. No points, accounts, automatic tracking or redemptions are active on this demonstration page. Points would expire 12 months after they are earned, subject to final published terms.

PROPOSED REWARD MECHANICS

Earn. Collect. Redeem.

1 Point for RM1

For every RM1 of eligible spending, a member would earn one Gaming Passport point. Eligible transactions and processing would need to be defined before launch.

EARN · 1 POINT / RM1

100 Points = RM5

Members would be able to exchange 100 points for RM5 in console credit. This is a proposed credit benefit, not a cash payout or a live voucher.

REDEEM · RM5 CONSOLE CREDIT

12-Month Validity

Gaming Passport points are proposed to expire 12 months after being earned. The full policy would be published and checked before implementation.

EXPIRY · 12 MONTHS
