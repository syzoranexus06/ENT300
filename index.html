ABOUT THE NEXUS

A proposed gaming space built for shared play.

Syzora Nexus is an ENT300 business proposal for a console-gaming arena serving students, young adults and groups. Our proposed concept focuses on accessible pay-per-hour sessions, comfortable shared entertainment and a digitally supported reservation experience.

The initial operating plan includes eight standard console stations and one private PS5 room. The proposed venue is in Seksyen 7, Shah Alam; exact premises, equipment specifications and operations remain subject to verification.
